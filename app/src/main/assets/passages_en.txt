# The corpus the Gesture Lab reads its passages from.

# One sentence per line, lowercase, letters and spaces only. Blank lines and lines starting
# with # are ignored, so this file can be grouped and annotated without the reader caring.
#
# Everyday message register on purpose: this keyboard is for short things said to people, and a
# threshold fitted to literary English is fitted to sentences this thumb will never type. The
# sentences are ordinary to the point of dullness, which is the property that matters -- a hand
# that has to think about what it is reading stops typing and starts performing.
#
# Every word here has to be in assets/lexicon_en.tsv or its glide can never decode correctly,
# and a bank full of unwinnable words reads as a broken decoder rather than a gap in the word
# list. PassagesTest checks the whole file against the lexicon.
